01 / ABOUT

The human side of the machine.

I started out designing interfaces. Now I study what happens when AI enters the loop — where it helps, where it misleads, and how to design it so people stay in control.

I like the overlap of interaction design, research and applied machine learning. I care less about what a model can do in a demo, and more about whether a student, a teacher or a customer can understand it, rely on it, and push back when it's wrong.
